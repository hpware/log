# Product roadmap

Implemented maintenance work:

- Owner-checked post editing, tag editing, and draft previews.
- Text, exact-tag, and post-type filters on home, profile, and search pages, preserved in URLs.
- Markdown feed previews with expandable text and safe links.
- Independent administrator feature toggles and server-version privacy.
- Collection creation, listing, title/slug editing, existing-post selection, deletion, and public collection pages.
- Built-in short share links for public and link-only posts.
- Media visibility checks, streaming, and video seeking.
- Custom video playback controls, expanded video dialogs, fullscreen, and accessible image dialogs.
- Account name and picture updates, plus password changes with current-password verification and session revocation.

Remaining product work:

- Video transcoding.
- Verified email changes and email-based password recovery (requires a mail provider).
- Additional administrator customization and external analytics integrations.
- Optional integration with an external Shlink service.
