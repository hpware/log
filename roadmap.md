# Product roadmap

Implemented maintenance work:

- Owner-checked post editing, tag editing, and draft previews.
- Working public profile tag filters, safe profile rendering, and text previews.
- Independent administrator feature toggles and server-version privacy.
- Collection creation, listing, deletion, adding newly published posts, and public collection pages.
- Built-in short share links for public and link-only posts.
- Media visibility checks, streaming, and video seeking.

Remaining product work:

- Render Markdown in feed previews (full post pages already support Markdown).
- Video overlay and custom playback controls (native controls are available).
- Search and home filtering controls beyond profile tag links and the public feed API.
- Video transcoding.
- Editing collection titles and membership of existing posts.
- Additional administrator customization and external analytics integrations.
- Optional integration with an external Shlink service.
